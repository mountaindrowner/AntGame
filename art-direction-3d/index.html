Ten game art styles for one ant nest

Every panel is a live Three.js diorama built from the same scene data: the same nest, the same ants, the same spider. Only the art style changes. Drag a panel to tilt the camera and see the nest in depth.

What to judge

  1. Can you tell worker, major, queen and a carrying ant apart at 1× and at 0.6× zoom?
  2. Does the nest read as a place where ants live, rather than a diagram?
  3. Does the style still read with 200 ants on a phone, and could it run there?
  4. Is the spider scary, funny, or neither? Each style has a right answer.
  5. Would you be happy looking at this for 20 hours?

Scene: 720×480 world units, rendered live with Three.js r147 (vendored). Styles live in style_<id>.js; the scene data is scene.js; shared geometry and rigs are in core.js.
