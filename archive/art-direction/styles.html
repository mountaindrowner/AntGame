What to judge

  1. Can you tell worker, major, queen and carrying apart at 1× and at 0.6× zoom?
  2. Does the nest read as a place where ants live, rather than a diagram?
  3. Does the scent trail read without a legend? (Only vector and HD pixel draw it.)
  4. Is the spider scary, funny, or neither? Each style has a right answer.
  5. Would you be happy looking at this for 20 hours?

Scene: 720×480 world px. Renderers live in render_<style>.js; the scene is scene.js. “1×” shows one world px per CSS px; “Fit” scales to the column.
