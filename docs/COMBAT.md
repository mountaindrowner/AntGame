## 2. Zones and guardians

The yard splits into **13 zones**. Each has one **guardian**: the thing that makes the zone dangerous.

**While the guardian lives:**
- its zone's regions can't be held;
- its hazards run at full strength;
- it shows on the map as a red crest over its lair.

**When it falls:**
- the zone's regions can be held;
- the zone's own hazard stops or eases (for example, no more robins over the lawn);
- you get a **trophy** with a lasting power, genes, and a feast of meat.

## 3. Guardians, fleshed out

Every guardian follows the same rules, so each fight is readable:

1. **A lair.** It lives in one spot. Walking into its zone the first time gives a banner with its name and title, and a short tell of how it fights.
2. **Telegraphs.** Every big move shows on the ground first (red shapes, as the bosses do now), long enough to dodge.
3. **A weakness.** Each has one thing that makes your crew's bites count double, so a smart crew beats a big one.
4. **Two phases.** At half health it changes: faster, new move, angrier.
5. **Its zone eases when it falls.** That is the reason to beat it.
6. **A trophy** with a lasting power, plus genes and a lot of meat.
7. **It heals if you leave.** A failed attack costs ants, not progress you can't get back.

### The Old Robin: Terror of the Great Lawn (zone 9). Built, build 30
This answers "what does the robin do?" Robins stay a hazard on the lawn, and the Old Robin is the one you can beat.
- **Lair:** the bare patch, in the middle of the lawn.
- **Moves:**
  - **Peck:** a red dot, then a strike that kills one ant.
  - **Hop:** it moves 4 tiles toward the biggest bunch of ants.
  - **Wing beat:** a ring that knocks ants back.
  - **Phase 2:** it takes off. Its shadow circles, then it swoops down a lane. It can't be bitten in the air.
- **Weakness: its feet.** Ants on its feet hold it down. With 6 or more on it, it is **pinned**: it can't take off or hop, and takes double bites.
- **Win:** you don't kill it. You drive it off: at 0 health it flies away for good, leaving a feather.
- **Zone eases:** no more robins anywhere on the lawn.
- **Trophy, Robin's feather:** your foragers are never taken by birds anywhere.

### Old Warty, the toad (zone 10). Built, build 31
- **Lair:** the toad hole.
- **Moves:**
  - **Tongue:** a long, thin lane shows, then the tongue grabs the first ant in it.
  - **Swallow:** a grabbed ant can be saved. If your crew bites the toad within 3 seconds, it spits the ant out alive.
  - **Leap:** a shadow, then it lands and squashes ants.
  - **Phase 2:** two tongues in a row, and it puffs up, so bites do half for 5 seconds.
- **Weakness: from behind.** It only sees in front. Bites from behind its head do double.
- **Its poison skin:** every bite costs the biter a little health. Medics nearby cancel that.
- **Zone eases:** no more toads on the wet edge.
- **Trophy, toad's poison:** your ants taste bitter. Anything that eats ants (toads, birds, spiders) kills half as many.

### The praying mantis (zone 11). Built, build 34
- **Lair:** the middle of the hedge. The hedge stops being "safe ground".
- **Moves:**
  - **Hidden:** you only see a shimmer until you are within 6 tiles.
  - **Strike:** it sways for 0.6 seconds (the tell), then snaps a cone in front, grabbing one or two ants.
  - **Feeding:** while it eats (4 seconds) its claws are busy.
  - **Phase 2:** it strikes twice in a row, and it flies short hops.
- **Weakness: when it's eating,** it takes double bites and can't strike.
- **Zone eases:** the hedge is safe again, and twice as fast to walk through.
- **Trophy, mantis claw:** each of your fighters' first bite in a fight always lands, for double.

### The black widow (zone 12). Built, build 35
- **Lair:** her web in the shed corner.
- **Moves:**
  - **Web:** sticky tiles around her, where ants get stuck for 3 seconds.
  - **Bite:** a short lunge at stuck ants. Lethal.
  - **Phase 2:** she drops on a thread to a new spot and spins fresh web.
- **Weakness: the web's four anchors.** Bite through them to drop her to the floor, where she can be swarmed and takes double bites.
- **Zone eases:** no more bug spray in the shed.
- **Trophy, widow's silk:** a web at your door. Raiders stick in it for 3 seconds.

### The giant centipede (zone 13)
- **Lair:** deep in the leaves.
- **Moves:**
  - **Coil:** a ring shows, then it strikes outward.
  - **Rush:** a fast run down a lane, biting everything in it.
  - **Body:** 6 segments, each with its own health. Each segment cut off makes it shorter, and faster.
  - **Phase 2:** at 2 segments left it goes frantic.
- **Weakness: its joints.** While it's coiled, bites do double.
- **Zone eases:** the leaf pile's protein can be taken safely. No more toads in the leaves.
- **Trophy, centipede fang:** Venom ants paralyse whatever they bite for 1 second.

### The yellowjacket nest (optional 14th, the Woodpile)
- **Lair:** a paper nest in the logs.
- **Moves:**
  - **Wasp sorties:** wasps fly out in waves and carry ants off.
  - **The nest:** has health, and only a crew at it can hurt it.
- **Weakness: rain.** Wasps can't fly in rain, so that is the moment to attack. This ties into the weather.
- **Zone eases:** no wasps anywhere.
- **Trophy, wasp paper:** builders build half again faster. The grubs inside are a huge protein haul.

## 4. Ant against ant: duels

Today, ants near each other just lose health every second. That is invisible and boring. It becomes **duels with dice**, with real ant fighting behind it: workers grab legs and pin, soldiers cut.

**The duel:**
- When a fighting ant meets an enemy within reach, the two **lock**. They face each other and their legs brace.
- Every **0.7 seconds** each rolls **1d6 + Attack**, against the other's **1d6 + Defence**.
  - **Beat it:** a hit, for the ant's bite damage.
  - **Beat it by 3 or more:** a hard hit, for half again.
  - **A natural 6:** a **crit**, for double. The target loses a leg and is slower for good.
  - **A tie:** **mandibles locked**. Nobody hits, and both are held for the next round.
- Both sides roll every round, so it goes back and forth. A soldier usually beats a forager, but not always.

**Ganging up (pinning):**
- Each extra ant on the same enemy gives its side +1 to hit, and the enemy −1 Defence.
- With 2 or more extra ants on it, the enemy is **pinned**: it can't run, and every hit on it counts as a hard hit.
- This is why crews matter, and why the swarming odorous ants are dangerous even though each one is weak.

**Starting numbers** (Attack / Defence):

| Ant | Attack | Defence | Special |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forager, builder, nurse, other workers | 0 | 0 | |
| Harvester | 1 | 1 | |
| Soldier | 2 | 2 | |
| Venom ant | 2 | 1 | Crits on 5 or 6 |
| Major | 3 | 4 | |
| Supermajor | 4 | 6 | Shrugs off pinning |
| Exploder | 1 | 0 | When it dies, it bursts: every enemy touching it loses a round |
| Your ant | 2 | 2 | Plus your upgrades |
| Odorous ant | −1 | −1 | Swarm: counts double when pinning |
| Pavement ant | 0 / 2 (soldier) | 0 / 2 | |
| Argentine ant | 0 / 1 | 0 / 1 | Fights to the death; never runs |
| Carpenter ant | 3 | 4 | |
| Fire ant | 2 | 1 | Sting: every hit burns 1 more over 3 seconds |

- **Ranks** add to this: Seasoned +1 Attack, Veteran +1 Defence, Elite +1 both, Legend +2 both.
- **Upgrades** (Strong mandibles, the beetle's shell and so on) turn into + to Attack or Defence. The old percentages go away.

**What you see:**
- A small clash spark each round.
- A little PS1-style number over a hit.
- **A crit:** a white flash, a "CRIT" glyph and a leg flung away.
- **Locked mandibles:** two ants shoving.
- **Your ant's duels:** both dice in a small readout by your health bar. "You 5+2 vs 3+2: hit!"
- **The battle report:** duels won and lost, crits, and who did best ("Thorn won 4 duels").

## 5. Raids: you raid other colonies

Raids should be the big moments. Today you only "hold the door until the queen dies". Instead, a raid has steps and a choice.

**1. Muster.** Recruit a crew and walk to a rival's door.
- A **Raid** button appears, as the Outpost button does now.
- A scout card shows what you're up against: about how many guards, their kind, the queen's health, and what they have (food, brood).

**2. Breach.** Press Raid.
- Their guards pour out; swarm colonies send everyone.
- At the door you fight duels.
- **Hold the door** (8 of yours there, none of theirs) **for 5 seconds** and it's open.

**3. Choose the prize** before your crew goes in:
- **Their store:** come out carrying food, up to half of what they hold.
- **Their brood:** come out carrying pupae, which hatch as **your workers**. This is the slave-maker idea from the War camp, and a War camp makes them hatch as soldiers.
- **Their queen:** go for the kill. Hardest, but the colony falls, and its zone's guardian goes with it.

**4. Inside.** The crew goes down the hole and the fight plays on a **raid card**.
- It's a small panel, round by round, with the same dice: your raiders against their nest guards, a line per round.
- "Thorn (Veteran soldier) rolls 6: crit! A guard falls."
- You can **pull out** at any round with what you've taken so far.
- At the queen, it's a duel against her: big health, high Defence, with guards that refill.

**5. Get home.** Survivors come out carrying the prize.
- They're slow while carrying, and the rivals chase them.
- Get them to your nest or an outpost and the prize is yours.

**Why raid more than once:**
- Each raid thins their numbers and their store, so the queen raid gets easier.
- Brood raids grow your colony.
- They **strike back**: a raided colony raids you within a few minutes. Their raids on you use the same duels at your door, and inside your nest if it's unguarded.

**The raid report** comes at the end: raiders lost, guards killed, crits, what you brought home, and the colony's state after ("Their numbers: 34 → 21").

## 5b. Crew orders (built, build 29)

- **Hold:** your crew guards 5 tiles round a spot.
- **Split:** half holds, half follows.
- **Follow:** call a holding crew back from anywhere.
- **Right-click:** on the ground, the crew goes there and holds; on an enemy, they attack it.

## 6. The Phase 1 end, redone

**Phase 1 ends when all 13 zone guardians have fallen.** That is all 13 main sections of the map and their main bosses, conquered.
- The rival colonies count in this, through their queens.
- The yellowjackets, if built, are a bonus, not required.
- **The map shows progress:** a crest per zone, red while the guardian lives and gold when it falls, and "Zones taken: 7 of 13".
- **The ending:**
  - the camera pulls up over the whole yard as each gold crest lights in turn;
  - your colony's record: days, ants raised, duels won, raids, guardians;
  - then the back door of the house swings open, and Phase 2 begins.
