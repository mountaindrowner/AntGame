Underfoot!

One queen. One rock. One very big yard.

A young queen lands in a backyard at dusk and has one night to get underground. You dig her first chamber, and her first egg hatches as you: one ant in a yard the size of a world.

Underfoot is an ant-colony game in the spirit of SimAnt, with a PlayStation-era look: wobbly low-poly, chunky pixels and fog. You play one ant out in the yard while you run the whole colony underneath it. Forage, lay scent trails, dig tunnels, raise rooms, recruit crews to haul home cookies and gummy bears, and fight your way across the yard to the rival nest on the patio.

Plays in your browser, on a phone or a computer. No install. Your colony saves itself as you play.

🐜 What you do

  • Be the ant. Tap to walk, pick up food and carry it home. Every trip lays a scent trail the colony follows.
  • Run the nest. Tap the queen to lay eggs: Foragers, Builders, Nurses, Soldiers and, if you can kill the spider, Venom ants.
  • Dig. Tap dirt to order tunnels. Builders carry every load of soil up and out to the spoil heap. Deeper soil is harder: packed earth needs a Workshop, clay needs a stronger queen.
  • Build rooms in the pockets you dig: a Hatchery, Food store, Cistern, Nursery, Barracks and Workshop.
  • Recruit a crew. Hold Recruit and lead your ants onto a treat too big for one ant, or onto a caterpillar for protein.
  • Take the yard. Kill the rival queen on the patio. Hold her door with eight fighters until she falls.

🌱 A very big yard

70 regions, waking up as you scout them. Each has its own food, its own dangers and its own prize:

  • the Back Steps and the Patio
  • the Flowerbed with the spider's web
  • the Compost Heap
  • the Sandbox's antlion pits
  • the Lawn and its mower
  • the Picnic Blanket
  • the Oak and its falling acorns
  • the hedge
  • the wet ground round the drain
  • the fire ants' mound in the far corner

A yard map fills in as you explore.

⚠️ Things that want you dead

  • A wolf spider that waits at its web and lunges
  • Rival pavement ants whose raids grow bigger and come for your queen
  • Feet (watch for the shadow) and robins that hunt the nearest ant
  • A toad with a long tongue, falling acorns and bug spray
  • The lawn mower, antlion pits and fire ants
  • Rain that washes out trails and caves in tunnels

🧬 Grow, evolve, remember

  • Genes. Earn them by playing: scouting new regions, bringing home a first-ever find, hatching eggs, killing big game, finishing goals. Spend them on an evolution tree of five branches: Bodies, Senses, Nest, Society and Symbiosis.
  • Upgrade every kind of ant. Each has its own levels, and some levels make you pick one of two abilities for good. Will your foragers be Pathfinders or Haulers? Will your soldiers be Doorkeepers or Escorts?
  • Veterans. Ants get better at their jobs as they work. They hatch pale and darken as they learn. Veterans learn a promotion, Elites earn a name, and the ant that kills the spider becomes a Legend.
  • Choose your rebirth. When you die, come back as one of your named Elites where it stands, or hatch again as any kind of ant you've unlocked. The fallen are remembered.
  • Outposts. Raise little cairns of pebbles far from home. Your foragers range round them. Beat a region's guardian and the region is yours, prize and all.
  • Goals. Three at a time, each paying Genes. Finish a whole set for a reward that lasts all game.

🎮 How to play

On a phone: tap to walk, and hold to keep walking. Buttons along the bottom pick up, lay a trail, recruit and release a crew, enter the nest and raise outposts. Hold any button to see its name.

On a computer: click to walk, and hover a button to see its name.

The intro walks you through your first night. After that, three tips:

  • 🌾 Food first. Every ant eats. Lay a Builder early, and build a Hatchery and a Food store.
  • 🥩 Soldiers eat protein. Hunt the caterpillar with a crew, or bring home dead bugs.
  • 🛡️ Guard your door before the first raid, around six minutes in.

Lost? The ? button has the rules. The ⋯ in the top bar shows your colony's numbers.

🔊 Sound

Every sound and the music are made live in your browser: footsteps, rain, the mower, the spider, a little marimba theme that shifts between the yard and the nest. Tap once to switch sound on. Music and effects can be set to off, quiet or loud in Options.
