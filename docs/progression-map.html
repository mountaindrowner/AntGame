The schedule

Eight tracks running side by side. Read down a column to see what a player is doing at that point; read across a row to see one track grow. Swipe sideways on a phone.

Unlock chains

How the tracks hook together: a boss drops a trophy, the trophy unlocks a caste, and the caste opens ground you couldn't cross before.

The evolution tree

Paid in 🧬 Genes, which come from playing: hatching (1 per 10 eggs), scouting new regions, bringing home new things, bosses, goals and rival queens. About 24 nodes; a yard run affords about two-thirds, so every colony differs. Each node has a deed that halves its cost. Nodes can be refunded, except the lineage.

Materials: one new one every 30 to 60 minutes

Each has its own way to get it and its own catch, so a new material is a new way of playing.
