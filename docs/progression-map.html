Every ant in the plan, what it grows into, and how each one levels up. Solid outlines are in the game today (build 11.6); dashed ones are proposed. Costs are first guesses for the bots to tune.

Ant family trees

Every new ant grows out of one you already have. The label above each one is what unlocks it: usually a boss's trophy plus a room. Tap an ant to jump to its upgrades.

Upgrade tree for each ant

Every ant type has three levels, bought with materials (never 🧬 Genes). Level 1 is a straight boost. Levels 2 and 3 add a boost plus a choice of two abilities. The choice is permanent for that colony, so two players' soldiers end up different.

The queen

Her level sets how many ants the colony can hold. Each satellite nest's queen adds 20 more.

Evolution tree

Paid in 🧬 Genes plus materials. Genes come from playing: 1 per 10 eggs hatched, 1 per new region, 1 per new kind of thing brought home, 2–5 per boss, 1–2 per goal, 5 per rival queen. Lines show what must come first. About 24 nodes; a yard run affords about two-thirds, so choose. Each node has a deed that halves its cost, and any node can be refunded except the lineage.

When it all arrives

The same pieces laid out by play time. Read down a column for what a player is doing then; read across a row to watch one track grow. Swipe sideways on a phone.

Materials

One new material every 30 to 60 minutes, each with its own way to get it and its own catch.
