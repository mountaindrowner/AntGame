# Playtest 3: Mark and his brother (Nov 6)

Mark's voice-over while his brother played, on PC, on build 67 or close to it. Pulled out of the transcript and sorted. Status tags:

- **fixed in 68:** changed in build 68.
- **exists:** the game has it; the answer is here.
- **next:** clear enough to build without a design call; proposed for the next builds.
- **Mark's call:** a design choice first.

## What landed well

- The attack controls ("the attack options do work well"); the attack animation he saw.
- The music ("not intrusive… perfect").
- The great evolutions, the region cuts on the map, Continue being instant.
- The nest feeling deep and expansive.
- The dark nights, play dead, the raid itself ("that was awesome").
- Names and veterans (Flint, Thorn "now an elite"); the millipede carried home in pieces.

## 1. Fixed in build 68

1. **Recruit and Release swap places.** The bottom row was one centred row; when Release appeared, everything shifted and Release landed where Recruit had been. Pick up, Trail, Recruit and Release now have fixed slots. Crew orders, Outpost, Raid and Enter grow to the right without moving them.
2. **Recruiting past the cap.** Each press was capped, not your whole crew, so pressing twice went over. Now the cap counts your whole crew, holding ants included.
3. **Tooltips off the bottom of the screen.** On a big monitor the HUD is scaled up, and the tip's position was scaled a second time. It now sits above the button. Tapped thing cards had the same bug.
4. **Dying without knowing.** Low health already had the red edge; now:
   - a heartbeat plays with it, faster below 20%;
   - hunger growls and shakes the hunger bar;
   - "Hungry" and then "Starving!" appear in the middle of the screen once each.
5. **The mower.** "Mower!" appears in the middle of the screen when it starts, instead of a notice at the side.
6. **Day and night.** A half-dial clock sits at the top centre, the way Mark described it after Warcraft III: day and night turn under it, with the sun or moon. At dusk and dawn it glows and says "Night falls" or "Dawn: dew on the grass" for a moment. It replaces the night and dawn banners. It is hidden on narrow phone screens, where the 🌙/☀️ chip in the top bar still counts down.
7. **Too many notices.** The how-to lines ("Tap the top cell to go outside. Tap the queen for eggs…", "lay an egg") now show only in a colony's first 15 minutes. Warnings (water, hunger, food) stay.
8. **Zooms out too far.** Now about three wheel notches less; the Zoom button's far step matches.
9. **Text too small in menus.** Every sheet and the Evolution window's text is about two points bigger, and the sheet is a little wider.

## 2. Questions from the session (exists)

- **Does eating drain the food?** No. You eat at the nest entrance or beside a forager carrying food, and nothing is taken from a food pile, the forager or the store.
- **Crew of 20:** the default. Bigger crews (30), Bigger crews II (45) and War columns (60) raise it, all evolutions.
- **The spider and spawn camping:** it can't stand near your nest (6 tiles; 12 in the first four minutes). It goes home to rest, wanders, sometimes leaves over the fence, and after three meals it's full for a minute. It has no day schedule.
- **The dirt pile on top** is the spoil your builders carry out of the nest. It's only for looks today; nothing uses it.
- **The "protein" bodies after a swarm** are winged queens and males that died in the nuptial swarm. Their card now says so (it said "Meat"). *fixed in 68*
- **The map's bottom right:** the zoom buttons. In the yard, that corner is Fire Ant Country (its crest and pin).
- **Can a builder you play dig?** No. Your taps queue digs for the builders. *Mark's call* (see 5).
- **Can a recruited crew carry water?** No; a crew follows and fights. *Mark's call*.

## 3. Next: creatures and combat (no design call needed)

1. **Predators that are a real threat.** Hunters (jumping spider, assassin bug, wasp, skink) beat a lone ant today, but a crew of five to eight drives them off. Ordinary foragers don't fight bugs at all. "They have to be genuine threats, not pathetic food sources": they should kill an ant or more before going down, and need the strongest ants or a real crew.
2. **The millipede runs** before it curls up.
3. **The spider fights back visibly:** a lunge, a rear and a slam. Ants attack its legs, spread around it, instead of piling under its body.
4. **Ants lunge when they bite.** Today a fighting ant only flickers its walk.
5. **Fights spread out:** ants pair off one-on-one in a loose front, not one cluster. Fights last longer, and the camera nudges in a little.
6. **The foot stomps harder:** more shake, more dust, a heavier thud.
7. **Rain fades in:** a few drips first.

## 4. Next: interface

1. **Hovering an ant outlines it**, every ant, with its name.
2. **A Defend button when the queen is attacked:** press it again and again (with a short cooldown) to call ants home. It's not automatic.
3. **The raid as a show:** a clearer view of what's happening, more animation and tension.
4. **The ant pages of the Evolution window:** fewer big bars, shorter text, the pages' tabs and arrows at the top.
5. **The top-left bars, bigger.**
6. **Save slots:** more than one nest.

## 5. Mark's call: the nest as a place

These change how the nest plays, so they need a decision first.

1. **Rooms need walls and a way in.** "Any smart player is just gonna dig out every bit of dirt." A room must be built in a chamber (soil round it, one or two doorways), not in open ground. Open digging should cost something, or room bonuses should need enclosure.
2. **Choose where a room goes** (today it goes in the nearest space that fits), and **drag to dig** (draw a tunnel, not cell by cell).
3. **Rooms grow when upgraded:** they look better and get a little bigger.
4. **Faster passages**, an upgrade.
5. **Fog below the first layer:** the deeper nest is hidden until you dig into it.
6. **Light in the rooms:** each room a little lamp (glow-worms, glowing fungus), so the nest isn't flat.
7. **Your ant digs** when it's a builder.
8. **A crew can pick things up** (water, food).
9. **Bioluminescence:** an evolution where your ants glow faint blue and leave faintly lit trails; or night vision.
