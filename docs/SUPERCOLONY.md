# The supercolony: where the game goes (draft, Nov 2)

A working plan from Mark's brainstorm after build 41. **Build 42 built the nest sections, depth bands, the bedrock, the 100-ant nest, the alerts at limits and the genes counter** (see DECISIONS.md). The rest is not built yet. Decided items are marked **Decided**; the rest are proposals to react to.

It extends `PROGRESSION.md` (Oct 29), which already sketched pieces of this:
- satellite nests, each with its own small nest (Track 2);
- the Royal chamber for mating flights;
- the great evolutions **Supercolony** (every nest joins into one colony) and **Swarm Day** (a nuptial flight founds three satellites at once).

Where this page and that one differ, this page is newer: daughter colonies are the satellites, and the nuptial flight comes partway through the bosses. Seasons stay shelved, as Mark decided on Oct 30; now they're a far maybe.

## The numbers behind it (build 41, bot campaign, 90 min)

| Zones taken | 1 | 13 | 8% |

**Can the tree be filled? Yes, but only by taking the whole yard.** Genes come almost entirely from one-time finds:

| Source | Genes |
|---|---|
| Scouting the 69 regions | ~69 |
| Holding regions | up to ~70 |
| 70 curios, plus 3 for every 5 found | ~112 |
| Goals | 53 |
| Guardians and bosses | ~32 |
| First foods and first kills | ~28 |
| Rival queens | ~17 |
| **Total** | **~380** |

On top of that, every 10 eggs hatched gives 1 gene, with no end.

Spending everything (evolution tree 274, caste upgrades 12) takes about 75% of that pool. So **filling the tree is the same as conquering the yard.** It isn't an accident, and it's a good shape. The player just needs to be told:
- "Genes found: 77 of about 380";
- where the rest are (the scouting, curio and guardian counts).

## Decided

**The nest:**
- **The edges are the brown space you see when zoomed out,** not the current 24-wide strip. The full nest is bigger. Rock or root walls are only its outermost edge.
- **The extra space opens in sections you buy,** like Fallout Shelter: a 10×10 or 5×5 block at a time. The price is food, or rarer things: curios, trinkets found in the yard, or genes.
- **Breaking into a section is an event,** not a queen level. Hidden things behind the walls are placed in advance: a root-aphid pocket, a beetle grub, an old tunnel.
- **Depth bands, where rooms work better:**
  - warm near the top: brood;
  - dry clay: stores;
  - cool and wet low down: fungus, the cistern, and the water table at the bottom.
  - Deep rooms are also safe from floods and the mower.
- **Bedrock at the very bottom can be dug, but each block takes a long time and costs a lot.** A few rare, great things are down there.
- **No second nest entrance.**

**Daughter colonies:**
- **Daughter colonies and satellite nests are the same thing.** It's a real nest you build up.
- **They open with a nuptial flight**, about a third to halfway through the bosses. That's not Phase 2.
- **A daughter colony is on its own until you connect it.** A tunnel to the main nest costs a lot. After that they share all their resources and your ant can travel between them.
- **They are never rivals.** Together they form a supercolony.
- **Each nest is capped at about 100 ants,** so growing past that means another nest.

**The map and the late game:**
- **The game slowly turns more macro.** Your main nest needs you less, you help the daughter colonies, and you come back to rally everyone for big fights.
- **The outer edge of the map is a wasteland tier, with the hardest bosses.** They need about three colonies working together. Ideas so far:
  - a colony taken over by cordyceps;
  - the Oak;
  - the worst of the bird and toad threats.

**Bosses, time and alerts:**
- **Bosses get character, later:** a name that fits what they do, and a look. For example, the robin as a scary, comedic villain with an eye patch.
- **Day and night: yes.** It gives the game a schedule. **Seasons: a far maybe.**
- **Alerts at every limit:** when you hit a ceiling, the game says so, and says what gets you past it.

## Daughter colonies: the plan of record (Nov 3)

Mark: daughters are **support colonies with a role, not second capitals**. Some of the tree's top nodes can only be built in the right daughter, and the biggest things only once a tunnel joins it to the main nest. **One daughter of each kind; five in all.**

### The five, one per branch of the evolution tree

| Daughter | Branch | Top nodes only buildable there | Its great evolutions | Natural ground |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Brood Hall** | Bodies | Polymorphism (Supermajors), Exploders, Long-lived workers, Lineage | Titan Line | sheltered soil: the flowerbed, the rot |
| **War Camp** | Society | Colony memory, Brood raiding, Honour guard | Army of the March; Brood Empire (with Brood Hall) | near a rival colony |
| **Watch** | Senses | Hive mind, War columns, Propaganda scent, Sun compass | Living Bridges (with Quarry) | high ground: the steps, the Oak roots |
| **Quarry** | Nest | Deep halls, Thermal chimneys, Highways, Shared stores | The Great Mound; Raft Colony (with Garden) | the rock garden, gravel |
| **Garden** | Symbiosis | Honey economy, Flower nectar, Fungus garden level III | Fungus Empire, The Peony Pact; Blue Butterfly Pact (with Watch); Weaver Canopy (with Watch) | the wet edge, the flowerbed |

**The tree gets three tiers:**
1. **Everything else:** buildable anywhere, as now.
2. **The top nodes above:** need that daughter; you build its room inside the daughter's nest.
3. **Great evolutions:** need the right daughters joined to the main nest by tunnel.
   - **Supercolony** needs all five joined.
   - **Swarm Day** needs the Royal chamber and two joined daughters.

**Saves:** nodes a save already owns stay owned.

### Revised by Mark (Nov 3, later): founding is a tunnel, and daughters lean on the main nest

1. **The nuptial flight comes at set moments in the story.** The winged queen picks a spot, lands at the outpost you chose and digs in.
2. **Then a tunnel is dug from the main nest to her,** slowly; the farther away, the longer.
   - On the world map, a **translucent line** grows from your nest to hers while it's dug.
3. **When the tunnel arrives, the daughter is built with a basic set of essentials** (room for more ants), fed and supplied from the main nest through the tunnel.
4. **You upgrade the essentials, then build the daughter's specialisations,** paid from the main nest.
5. **Daughters depend on the main nest.** Some can't defend themselves, and need you (or the War Camp) to defend them. The total number of ants should still go up with each daughter.

**What this changes:**
- The tunnel is part of founding, not the late "big goal".
- Great evolutions need the daughter's specialisations, not a tunnel.

**Also decided:**
- **A short loading animation between nests is fine.** Tapping a nest's picture plays about a second of travel through the tunnel while its nest and ants load.
- **Lore:** at the very bottom of the nest, the bedrock has **faintly glowing green rocks.** It's a hint of something radioactive below, and of why these ants grow. It's subtle, and you only see it if you dig all the way down.

### Founding, size and limits

- **The way in:** the main nest's Royal chamber node, plus about 4–5 guardians beaten, brings the **nuptial flight**. Each flight's winged queen founds one daughter.
- **Where:**
  - at a finished outpost you hold;
  - at least **two regions from every nest**, so the walk there hides the loading;
  - within reach of an existing nest, so expansion is a chain (as SimAnt did it).
- **Its role:** you pick the role at founding; the ground gives a bonus when it suits the role.
- **Its nest:**
  - small, about a third of the main one;
  - comes pre-dug for its role;
  - you place only its two or three special rooms;
  - you must be inside it to build them, which gives you reasons to visit.
- **Size:** up to ~50 ants each. With the main nest's 100, that's at most ~350, of which only the main nest and the daughter you're at run in full.
- **Running:** each daughter runs itself.
  - It lays its role's ants, forages and spends its own stores, and guards its door.
  - Alerts always name the colony and offer to take you there.
  - You can step in and override; automation is on by default (the Stellaris lesson).
- **The capital decides:** genes, the evolution tree and queen traits stay with the main nest and apply to all colonies (as in Frostpunk 2).
- **If the main queen dies:** still game over, unless the Eternal line trait. *(Assumed; Mark to confirm.)*

### What each gives you

| Daughter | What it gives |
|---|---|
| Brood Hall | the big castes, trained there |
| War Camp | elite fighters who march to your raids and guardian fights |
| Watch | the map revealed further, early raid warnings, longer outpost reach |
| Quarry | stone, and deep and bedrock digging |
| Garden | sugar and food, shipped home once a tunnel connects it |

### Tunnels: the big goal

- **What one costs:** a lot, rising with distance.
- **What it does:**
  - the two nests share stores;
  - you travel through it (in at one nest, out at the other);
  - its great evolutions unlock.
- **What comes later:** all five joined is the supercolony, and the way into the wilds.

### Keeping it fast (from how other games do it)

- **Far away, a daughter is numbers, not ants:** count, food in and out, losses, catching up on elapsed time; never frozen. It fills in with real ants a region before you arrive (simulation level of detail; Dwarf Fortress, Oxygen Not Included).
- **Idle ants sleep:** ants resting inside a nest skip their update (Factorio).
- **Decisions are spread across ticks:** movement every tick, choices on a rolling share of ants (time slicing).
- **Only one nest is ever drawn.** Going into a daughter swaps the nest view to its small grid.
- **New art is small:**
  - a mound look per role: war banners, leaf cuttings, a stone pile, a lookout twig, egg-pale walls;
  - role icons;
  - the tunnel line on the map.

### The builds, in order

1. ~~**Speed first**~~: built in build 45. It's about 3× faster with many ants, and byte-identical in behaviour. Spreading decisions is held in reserve.
2. **The nuptial flight and the first daughter (War Camp):**
   - founding at an outpost;

## The open decision: open sandbox, or a guided path?

**Proposal: a guided path through the middle, with freedom in how you walk it.**
- The things Mark wants already need a path: 100 ants per nest, bosses that need three colonies, and a supercolony at the end. They only work if the game knows roughly where the player is.
- How you get there stays open: which zones first, which build, which rooms.

A draft of the path:

| Stage | Roughly | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Founding | 0–45 min | One nest, the home zones, the first guardians. Buy the first nest sections. |
| 2. The yard | 45–120 min | Zone guardians. Taking about 5 of the 13 zones brings the **nuptial flight** and the first daughter colony. |
| 3. The supercolony | 2–4 h | Two or three daughter colonies. Tunnels join them, they share resources, and your ant travels between them. Each nest gets jobs. |
| 4. The wilds | late | The wasteland tier. Bosses that need three colonies at once. Beating them ends Phase 1, and the house (Phase 2) opens. |

**Still to decide:**
1. **Grow the map, or reuse what's there?**
   - The yard isn't being used up: 7% is seen at 90 min. So the wasteland could be the outer regions that already exist (the Oak's 6, Fire Ant Country, the Rot, the Wet Edge) promoted to the hardest tier, instead of a new ring.
   - A new ring adds space no one will reach for hours.
2. **How many nests in all?** For example, the main nest plus three daughter colonies, each up to 100 ants.
3. **What can be spent on nest sections:** food only, or also curios, trinkets or genes.
4. **When the nuptial flight comes:** after a number of zones, or after named bosses.

## Alerts at limits (small, could come first)

A one-time card for each limit you hit, saying what gets you past it. It comes back only after the limit has eased for a while.

| Limit | The card says |
|---|---|
| Ant cap | "The queen can't hold more ants. Upgrade her (queen sheet) or found a daughter colony." |
| Food or water cap | "The store is full. Build or upgrade a Food store or Cistern." |
| Nest full: no room fits the dug space | "No room to build. Buy the next section." |
| Bottom of the open nest reached | "Deeper is clay: it needs …" |
| Genes piling up (15+ unspent) | "You have 🧬 15. Open Evolve." |
| Starving | Already shown; also say where food is. |
| Guardian ready to fight but your crew is too small | "Too strong for 6 ants. Bring 15." |
