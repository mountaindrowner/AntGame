## The rules every room follows

- **Unlocked by evolution.** Each new room comes from a node in the evolution tree. Later rooms sit deeper in the tree, behind other nodes. Until it's evolved, the room shows in the Build list, greyed, with the node to evolve.
- **A room trains its ant.** Each new kind of ant can only be laid once its room is built, the way Soldiers need a Barracks today.
- **Each room has its own levels, II and III,** bought from the Build list once the room stands. Level III usually costs 🪵 wood.
- **The existing caps still apply:**
  - one of each room per queen level
  - each extra costs more
  - every room eats a little food to run

## Wood

- **What it is:** a new material from 🪵 twigs and bark chips.
- **Where it turns up:** on bark, mulch, leaves, hedges and around the Oak.
- **Who fetches it:** foragers and builders, on a new "Fetch wood" duty.
- **What it pays for:**
  - the queen's last level
  - the builder's last upgrade
  - most rooms' level III

  It is also the material the Royal chamber and Highways will need.
