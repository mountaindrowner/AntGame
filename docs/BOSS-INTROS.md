# Boss introductions (build 78)

- **Asked:** "Little boss animations whenever there's a for sure boss… everything stops and the ants turn their attention to the boss and the boss turns their attention… an introduction sequence when a boss is introduced, at least when they're in fighting range. Maybe the same thing with the big enemy ants."
- **Means:** the first time you're about to fight a guardian, the game should stop for a beat and make it an event. Time freezes, every ant looks at it, it looks back, it roars, its name lands. It has to be quick, never in the way.
- **Today:**
  - The first time you come within 18 tiles of a boss, a purple card shows its name and how to beat it, with a sound and a small shake.
  - Nothing stops, nobody reacts, and it often lands while the boss is still asleep off-screen.
  - A war party's champion has only its health bar.
- **Others:**
  - **Monster Hunter:** each monster's first appearance is a short cutscene. The camera finds it, it roars, its name card slams in, then you play.
  - **Hollow Knight and Dark Souls:** no cutscene, but the boss's name and title in big letters as the fight begins.
  - **Warcraft III and StarCraft II:** a hero's arrival gets a camera pan, a voice line and a held beat.
  - The common rules:
    - it's short (two to four seconds);
    - it happens once per foe;
    - the fight starts at the end, not before;
    - you can skip it.
- **Design: the introduction, about three and a half seconds, once per boss per colony.**
  1. **Freeze.** The game pauses (the sim stops, and the scene keeps drawing). Black bars slide in top and bottom, the music ducks, and a low drum hits.
  2. **Everyone looks.** Every ant within sight turns to face the boss and gives a small start. The boss turns to face your ant.
  3. **The camera finds it.** It glides from your ant to the boss and eases in.
  4. **It roars.** Its roar sounds, a ring of dust bursts from it, and the screen shakes.
  5. **Its name lands.** A title card slams in: its name, its title, and one line on how to beat it.
  6. **Back to play.** The camera glides back, the bars slide away, and the boss song starts.
  - **When:** the first time you're within fighting range (22 tiles) of a boss that's awake and fighting. While it's asleep you only get a line in the feed and its ominous sound, so the introduction comes when it matters.
  - **Skip:** any click or key ends it at once.
  - **Not while you're in the nest,** a menu is open or another introduction is playing. It waits until you're out and near.
  - **The big enemy ants:** a war party's champion gets the same introduction when you first come within 22 tiles. It raises its mandibles, its war party turns with it, and its card reads "Champion of the pavement ants".
